# 🧾 1. Balance Overview Cards (Top Section)

Type: Simple summary cards (no chart, but with color indicators)

Total Balance – user’s lifetime savings.

Monthly Balance – remaining funds this month (income - expenses).

Total Income This Month

Total Expenses This Month

✅ Keep these as simple numeric cards with icons or small arrows showing ↑↓ percentage change vs. last month.

📊 2. Monthly Balance Trend Chart

Type: Line chart (or area chart)

Compares:
👉 Monthly income vs expenses across time (last 6 months or year)

X-axis: Month (Jan → Oct)
Y-axis: Amount in currency
Lines:

Income (green line)

Expenses (red line)

💡 Shows spending habits over time and which months overspent.

🧭 3. Category Distribution (Spending Breakdown)

Type: Pie or Doughnut chart

Compares:
👉 Share of total expenses by category for the current month

Example:

Food 🍔 – 35%

Rent 🏠 – 25%

Transport 🚗 – 15%

Shopping 🛍️ – 10%

Others – 15%

💡 Lets users visually see where most of their money goes.

🕓 4. Expense vs Income Progress Bar (Monthly Summary)

Type: Horizontal progress bar or gauge

Compares:
👉 Total monthly income vs spent amount

Example:
If user earned $2000 and spent $1200, show progress bar filled 60%.

💡 Helps instantly see how much of the monthly budget is used.

📈 5. Category Trends (Optional but Powerful)

Type: Small multi-line or stacked bar chart

Compares:
👉 Each category’s spending trend over the last 3–6 months

💡 Example: user can see that Food expense is increasing each month.
